# 6-Month Fitness Plan

Only 4 exercises: **push-ups, pull-ups, squats, crunches**. Plus jump rope.

## How it works (split routine)

Each big muscle group is hit **only once per week** — so it gets 7 days of rest.

- **Mon — Push day:** push-ups + crunches
- **Tue:** walk or rest
- **Wed — Pull day:** pull-ups + crunches
- **Thu:** jump rope or walk
- **Fri — Legs day:** squats + crunches
- **Sat / Sun:** rest

## Every workout

1. Warm-up 5 minutes (arm swings, leg swings, easy push-ups)
2. The main exercise — X sets × Y reps, rest 90 sec (push-ups & squats) or 2 min (pull-ups)
3. Crunches — X sets × Y reps, rest 60 seconds
4. Cool-down 5 minutes

## Test week (Week 22)

One exercise per day, find your max.

- **Mon:** max push-ups in one set
- **Tue:** rest
- **Wed:** max pull-ups in one set
- **Thu:** rest
- **Fri:** max squats in 2 minutes
- **Sat:** max crunches in 2 minutes
- **Sun:** rest

Write the numbers down. Compare to week 0.

## Rules

1. If you can't hit the rep count — do what you can and move on. Don't grind ugly reps.
2. Miss a day → push the week forward by 1 day. Don't double up.
3. Sleep 7+ hours. Eat protein. Drink water.
